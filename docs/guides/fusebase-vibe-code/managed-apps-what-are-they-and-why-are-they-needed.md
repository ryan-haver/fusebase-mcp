This guide is a short walkthrough.

In the list of apps, you may notice some that you did not create and that are marked with the “Manage” label. These are apps created by the Fusebase team, which serve both as examples of what can be built in FuseBase and can also be used for business purposes.

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-1024x290.png)

By default, several Manage apps are available, with a particular focus on the Business Apps pack. This is a comprehensive set of apps designed for use in service-based businesses: here you can find invoice generators, proposal tools, and solutions for creating complete contracts.

The business apps are tightly integrated with Fusebase, allowing you, for example, to access client information, add new clients, and more.

Manage apps are currently provided as-is, without the ability to modify their functionality.
