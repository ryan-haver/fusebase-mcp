FuseBase Business/Teams accounts have a higher level of management which can be run in the **Organization Console**. Through it you can create organizations, manage workspaces and members, add your branding to public pages and web clients, and much more.

## Types of accounts in organizations

-   Organization **Owner** and **Managers** have access to all workspaces and to the organization’s console.
-   Organization **Members** can access the workspaces they have been added to by the organization **Owner or** **Managers**.
-   Members with a **Guest** role can have access to only one workspace and their stay in the organization can be limited in time.

**[Read more about Guests Accounts](/guides/organization/guest-accounts)**.

**☝Note: A member can be a part of an organization, but not be a part of a workspace.**

![Nimbus Business/Teams accounts have a higher level of management which can be run in the Organization Console. ](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/CbT26xKs0nGQOG2Q/Group 5 (8).png)

## Types of permissions in workspaces

-   **Admin** – users can edit pages, add new pages, share your pages through public pages.
-   **Can create and edit** – users will be able to do full-fledged work on pages – read, edit, and even delete pages. Folders and tags management will also be available.
-   **Read only** – user’s pages will be available only for reading.

![Types of permissions in workspaces](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/LVuEWZI9bxhMJaev/Group 26 (3).png)

## Add members in the organization

![Add members in the organization](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/iT2r2nrLG6zYnOSU/without.gif)

To add a member to the organization, follow these steps.

-   Go to [https://teams.nimbusweb.me/](https://teams.nimbusweb.me/).
-   Navigate to **Members** – Invite new members.
-   Enter the member’s email address.
-   Choose a nessesary role in the organization.
-   Select the workspace to which you want to add the member. If you want to add a guest or a member without a workspace, select “**without workspace**.”  
    In the future, you can manually add guests / members to the desired workspace through the workspaces page.
-   Select permissions for the user.
-   Send an invite.
-   The user will receive an email with an invitation to join. After accepting, the user will become a member of the organization.

**☝Note: An invite is sent only the first time a member is added to the workspace. In the future, the member will be automatically added to the workspace.**

![An invite is sent only the first time a member is added to the workspace. In the future, the member will be automatically added to the workspace.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/KwgWUdkwadlenzSI/Group 2 (17).png)

## Add members in the workspace

![Add members in the workspace](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/Ex8tJK7SFdiDLoU7/2 (2) (1).gif)

To add a member directly to the workspace, you need to:

-   Go to the section **Workspaces** – **Manage**.
-   Next, select the workspace to which you want to add the user.
-   Press on **+** in the Members section or select **Invite members** inside the three dots menu.
-   Select **New by email** and enter the user’s email address.
-   Choose a nessesary role in the organization.
-   Select permissions for the user.
-   Send an invite.
-   The user will receive an email with an invitation to join. After accepting, the user will become a member of the organization.

**☝** **Note: After adding a member to the workspace, you can always change their permissions.**

![Add members in the workspace](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/ASBqe5fsMeVC7ROL/Group 26 (3).png)

**☝** **Note: You can also add a member to the workspace from existing members.**

![You can also add a member to the workspace from existing members.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/FdsgQTmL8Ly6cpko/Group 28 (3).png)

### Remove from workspace

To remove a member from the workspace, you need to:

-   Click on the three dots menu.
-   Select **Remove from workspace**.

![After being removed from the workspace, the user is NOT removed from the organization.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/ztpZhBG51SSP6cqx/Group 33 (1).png)

**☝** **Note: After being removed from the workspace, the user is NOT removed from the organization.**

## Manage Members in organization

In the Members section, you can add members to your organization and manage them.

### Edit user profile

![Edit user profile](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/t0JrIA14z6z0XGXu/1 (2).gif)

To manage you need to go to the Members section.

-   Select **Manage**.
-   Then click on the member.
-   The window for editing data will appear on the right. In it you can change:
    -   the role (Manager/Member)
    -   First name
    -   Last name
    -   Username
-   To change the profile photo, hover over the avatar and click **Upload**

![To manage you need to go to the Members section.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4516842/y3meroodx7crcb0b4pgq/nObtloHAdyL18ax0/Group 16 (3).png)

### Delete from organization

To remove a member from the organization, you need to:

-   Go to the **Manage** section.
-   Click on the three dots menu and select **Delete from organizatio**n.
-   Also you can click on the member and select **Delete from organization**.
