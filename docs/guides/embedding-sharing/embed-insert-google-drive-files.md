Embeds appear as a part of a page, but they are actually integrations with other sites. Use them to access another site or tool and work with it right in [Fusebase](https://thefusebase.com/).

Unite embeds from different platforms on a single FuseBase page: Google Analytics, Figma, Calendly and many more to skyrocket your pages’ functionality and keep all your information in one place!

## Embed from popular sites like YouTube, Google Maps, Google Spreadsheets

Below are some example inserts from Youtube, Google Maps, and Google Spreadsheets

-   Click on the arrow and select **Embed** or **cmd/ctrl** + **⌥/alt** + **m**
-   Paste or type the URL

**☝** **Note: to insert and edit you need to set the right permissions. For example, if you want to share a Google Docs file with your teammate, you need to make sure that he/she has access on Google Docs. Optionally, you can share your Doc or Sheet as a Public link, so everyone has access to it.**

## Resize embeds

The size of the embed can be changed. Just follow these steps.

-   Drag the upper-right corner and **resize** (all proportions will be preserved).

![The size of the embed can be changed. Just follow these steps.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4465536/tie77b7q6qdbekbas2h1/gojc2d6sAC590H0S/Group 148.png)

-   For free resizing, hold the **ALT key** and change the size using the bottom-right chip.

![For free resizing, hold the ALT key and change the size using the bottom-right chip.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4465536/tie77b7q6qdbekbas2h1/cB6vhHcFf7PU3wvO/Group 147.png)

**☝** **Note: To return the embed to its original position, click the three dots and select Reset size.**

### Lifehack

You can also enable full-width mode by clicking on the embed menu and activating **Full Width**.

## You can connect your Google Drive account and insert links to your drive files

Also you can easily insert a file from Google Drive

![Also you can easily insert a file from Google Drive](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4465536/tie77b7q6qdbekbas2h1/hnX0ywE3DGzk7Ym3/Google_docs (1).gif)

-   Click on the drop down menu and select **Google Drive** OR **cmd/ctrl** \+ **⌥/alt** + **g**

![Click on the drop down menu and select Google Drive OR cmd/ctrl + ⌥/alt + g](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4465536/tie77b7q6qdbekbas2h1/1pvCw49Df3nI8AAY/Group 1 (16).png)

-   Log in to your account and give permission to FuseBase

![Log in to your account and give permission to Nimbus Note](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4465536/tie77b7q6qdbekbas2h1/03DuHC1EYTfH9LNl/Group 3 (1).png)

-   Select the file you want to insert and click on **Select**

![Select the file you want to insert and click on Select](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/4465536/tie77b7q6qdbekbas2h1/MnaDZ9YuaSF3mDTk/Group 5 (1).png)
