## What Types of Pages Export Are in FuseBase?

You can export pages in HTML and PDF formats.

## How to Export Pages?

1) Open the desired page.

2) From the page menu, choose **Download**:

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-1.png)

3) Click on the desired format. If you pick PDF, it will conserve text + pictures. If you choose HTML, the archive will be saved, where in addition to the HTML file, you will also find note designs, images and files (if they remain in the note).

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-2-1024x493.png)

4) Save the file!

Important! If the page file is large or there is a big export queue, it might take some time to process the note.
