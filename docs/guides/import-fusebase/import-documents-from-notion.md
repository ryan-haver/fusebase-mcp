## Export pages from Notion

-   Log into your **Notion account**.
-   Open the desired Workspace.
-   Select **Export** from the menu.

![Export pages from Notion](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5533621/io4i2i41hnzugshwcx9a/arP2z3KRDqolJQK8/Group 218.png)

-   Select **HTML** and click on **Export**.

![Export pages from Notion](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5533621/io4i2i41hnzugshwcx9a/Omsxco7YQ4OAnVEN/Group 211 (1).png)

-   Save the exported file.

## Import pages to Nimbus

![Import pages to Nimbus](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/box/attachment/5533621/io4i2i41hnzugshwcx9a/JcFgNryxP66xSmvb/notionn_3.gif)

-   Open [Nimbus Note](https://thefusebase.com/note/).
-   In the import menu, click on **Import from Notion**.

![Import pages to Nimbus](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5533621/io4i2i41hnzugshwcx9a/1VyGZLakpwpJ1NL9/Group 212 (1).png)

![Import pages to Nimbus](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5533621/io4i2i41hnzugshwcx9a/pQdDAkOpGZiNyvyx/Group 209 (2).png)

-   Select the exported **HTML file**.
-   Wait for the import to finish.
