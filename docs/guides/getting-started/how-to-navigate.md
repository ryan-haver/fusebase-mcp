## Navigate through workspaces and organization

If you have several workspaces been invited to a friend’s/colleague’s workspace,or invitedto another organization, you can easily switch between them.

-   Click on your **account avatar**. Here you will see a list of the organizations you are a member of.

-   In the left sidebar, you will see the **list of workspaces** where you’re a member. Click on the desired one to go to the workspace.

![How to navigate](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Frame-2147207152-6.png)

## Sidebar and Folders list

The sidebar displays lists the folders and pages in the current workspace. Clicking on a folder expands its structure and shows the subfolders and pages that are in that folder:

![How to navigate](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Sidebar-and-Folders-list.gif)

## All Pages

If you want to see all the pages that are in the workspace navigate to **All Pages**:

![How to navigate](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Frame-2147207153-5-1024x476.png)

## Folders - list of all folders in workspace

Clicking on **Folders** will display a list of all root folders in the workspace:

![How to navigate](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Folders-list-of-all-folders-in-workspace-1024x438.png)

Clicking on a folder will reveal its contents (subfolders and pages).
