# Welcome Message & Search Management via Hero Section

Read this guide to learn tips for managing the Hero section to customize the Welcome Message and the Search box of your Client Portal.

The **Hero Section** is a block in the portal that includes a welcome message and a search block:

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Hero-screen-01-1024x475.png)

In FuseBase portals you can:

-   Change the text of the Welcome Message
-   Hide the Welcome Message & Search block

## Changing the Welcome Message

[Embedded content](https://www.youtube.com/embed/KQl8GdsBVwo?feature=oembed)

1) Go to the **Portal Customizer**.

2) Hover over the Hero Section and click on **Edit**

3) **Type in** the text you want

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Hero-Screen-03-1024x489.png)

4) Save your changes and **Publish** them

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Hero-Screen-04-1024x450.png)

## Hiding Welcome Message & Search

[Embedded content](https://www.youtube.com/embed/MowzySYBRUI?feature=oembed)

1) Go to the **Portal Customizer**.

2) Hover over the Hero Section and click **Edit**.

3) Click on **No Content**.

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Hero-Screen-05-1024x499.png)

4) Save and **Publish** your changes.

If you want to return to the Hero Section, hover over the empty space and click **Edit**. Then select **Greeting+Search** and save your changes.
