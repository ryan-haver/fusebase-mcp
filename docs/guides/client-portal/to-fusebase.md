# Switching from the Customiser

You can visit the FuseBase directly from your portal. Just click on "To FuseBase" and it redirects you to FuseBase in the new tab.

In this guide

1.  [Go to the FuseBase workspace from the Portal Customiser](#b576038340_1391)

You can manage portal content from both the **Customiser** and the **FuseBase** client. In the client itself, the entire portal structure is displayed in the sidebar.

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Customiser-and-the-FuseBase-client-1024x438.png)

Here you can

-   Edit the content of a page created in FuseBase Editor (click on the page and start editing).

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/FuseBase-Editor-1024x437.png)

-   Open a page created in the Portal Editor in the **Customiser** (click on the page).
-   Add a new page or folder by clicking on the **plus** or **Add item**.

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/plus-or-Add-item-1024x418.png)

-   Go to the Customiser by clicking the **Visual Editor**.

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Visual-Editor-1024x646.png)

## Go to the FuseBase workspace from the Portal Customiser

When working in the **Portal Customiser**, you can always return to the **FuseBase workspace** by clicking the **Back button** in the top bar of the **Customiser**.

![](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Portal-Customiser-1024x400.png)
