# Member menu – allows members to manage their profile

You can add a member menu to the portal, which will allow clients to:

-   manage their profile on the portal
-   sign out of their account

You can enable or disable the menu in the Top Bar settings.

Once you activate the menu, portal clients will have the following options:

-   change their first and last name for the portal
-   update their avatar
-   change their password
-   sign out of their account
-   etc.
