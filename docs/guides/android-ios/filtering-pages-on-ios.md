# Filtering pages on iOS

In Nimbus Note, you can filter pages by colored labels and upcoming reminders.

In [Nimbus Note](https://thefusebase.com/note/), you can filter pages by colored labels and upcoming reminders.

![In Nimbus Note, you can filter pages by colored labels and upcoming reminders.](/hosting-assets/legacy/thefusebase/box/attachment/5163319/y3320oh6yk9rvw649a0s/ZGrZceclgrGs6wqE/1983_filteriOS.gif)

To filter pages, you need to:

-   Click the **Sort and Filter** icon.
-   Select the desired type of filtering and sorting.

![In Nimbus Note, you can filter pages by colored labels and upcoming reminders.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5163319/y3320oh6yk9rvw649a0s/Xx0NK6HSpPYu0RFC/Group 133 (1).png)

## Filtering by colored labels

In the filter menu, select the desired color and click on it, then you will see a list of pages with the selected color.

![In the filter menu, select the desired color and click on it, then you will see a list of pages with the selected color.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5163319/y3320oh6yk9rvw649a0s/dbiEAitZutFKsDDq/Group 134 (1).png)

**☝Note: If you create a pages during the active color filtering, the selected color is automatically assigned to it.**

To reset the filters, open the filter menu again and click **All Pages**, or tap on the selected color in the filter again.

## Filtering by reminders

You can also see the list of pages with upcoming reminders.

To do this, tap on **Upcoming reminders** in the filter menu. You will see a list of pages with reminders that are sorted by date: from nearest to farthest.

![You can also see the list of pages with upcoming reminders.](https://thefusebase.com/box/attachment/5163319/y3320oh6yk9rvw649a0s/yX8h8Rglk6aYV5PH/Group 135 (1).png)

**☝Note: To reset the filters, open the filter menu again and click All Pages.**
